01 1015 Read mem to 15
02 3015 Add value in 15 to Acc
03 2114 Store Acc in mem 14
04 2013 Load value mem 13 to Acc (counter)
05 3112 Sub from Acc value in mem 12 (1)
06 4210 Jump if Acc = 0
07 2113 Store counter in 13
08 2014 Load Sum into Acc
09 4000 Jump to 00 cell
10 1114 Print sum (value in 14)
11 4300 Exit

13 0010 Counter

06 3116 Sub from Acc value in mem 16 (1)
07 4211 Jump if Acc = 0 (if counter = 0)

12 4300 Exit
